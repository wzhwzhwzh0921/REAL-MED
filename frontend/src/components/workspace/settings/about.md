# REAL-MED

Benchmarking LLM Agents on Real-World Medical Tasks.

405 tasks across Clinical Support, Patient Management, Pharmacy Management,
and Medical Education & Research. Agents submit documents, spreadsheets,
presentations, or simulated HIS operations, evaluated with expert rubrics.

[Source and evaluation instructions](https://github.com/wzhwzhwzh0921/REAL-MED).

Built on DeerFlow, LangChain, and LangGraph. Upstream license notices are retained.
